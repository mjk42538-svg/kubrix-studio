# Lessons (read first)
1. Views come in, but subscribers don't: 5,011 views → 12 subs (0.24%). Every episode needs a reason to follow: a series with a cliffhanger, the same hero, "Part 2" said out loud before the last 5 s, and a pinned comment promising the next part.
2. Trend-based premises did best (HUSS party = top Short). Tie premises to what kids talk about this week.
3. 44% swipe in the first seconds → frame 0 = the most extreme image + the rule in under 2 s + 2–4 words of on-screen text at frame 0.
4. Volume: 4 public videos in a month is too few for the Shorts feed to learn who to show us to → aim for 1 a day (render ≈2 h/video, so the bottleneck is voice recording).
5. Keep series parts 60–75 s (Monkey King P1 is 1:40; watch its % viewed against the ~60 s videos).
