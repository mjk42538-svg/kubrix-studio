Last time, every monkey was gone. All I found were claw prints... bigger than my whole body.
I followed them to a cave. Inside: the Demon King of Havoc.
He laughed. "You're the Monkey King? You're the size of a peanut!"
So I pulled out my own hair... and blew. Every hair turned into a tiny me. Hundreds of them.
They pinched him, poked him... and I bonked him with his own sword.
I saved every monkey. But I needed a real weapon.
Under the ocean, the Dragon King kept a treasure no one could lift.
He gave me a sword. Too light. A spear. Too light. A giant fork. Snapped it like a breadstick.
Then the palace started shaking. A golden iron pillar. Glowing. Waiting for me.
Eight tons. And it could change size.
"Smaller!" A needle. "Bigger!" It touched the ocean floor.
I tucked it behind my ear. "Thanks. I'll take it."
That night, two men in black chained me and dragged me to the Underworld.
They opened the Book of Life and Death. My name: "Sun Wukong. Dies... tonight."
So I grabbed their brush... and crossed it out. Mine, and every monkey's. Forever.
Up in heaven, the Jade Emperor slammed down his tea. "Bring me... that monkey."
