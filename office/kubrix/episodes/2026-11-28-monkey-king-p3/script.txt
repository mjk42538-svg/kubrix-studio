A hundred thousand soldiers are surrounding my house. And honestly? I deserve it.
It started with a job offer. From heaven. To me, the monkey who erased his own death.
The Jade Emperor smiled. "Welcome! You'll be... Keeper of the Heavenly Horses."
I got a fancy hat. A fancy desk. A thousand horses. I was so proud.
Then a guard laughed. "That's the lowest job in heaven. You're a stable boy."
Stable. Boy.
I kicked over the desk, flew home, and painted a giant flag: "Great Sage, Equal to Heaven."
Heaven sent an army. I sent them back... upside down.
So the Emperor tried again. "Fine! Guard my Peach Garden."
These peaches grow once every three thousand years. Eat one... and you live forever.
I ate one. Then another. Then... all of them.
Then I found a party nobody invited me to. So I drank all their juice.
Then I walked into Laozi's lab and ate his pills of immortality. Like candy.
I woke up. Burped. And the sky turned gold.
Which brings us back to the hundred thousand soldiers.
Part four: me versus all of heaven. Follow so you don't miss it.
