Being busy is the laziest thing you will ever do.
I know. You worked twelve hours today. Forty-seven emails. Six meetings. You're exhausted.
But be honest. What actually moved?
Here's the ugly truth. Busy is where smart people hide.
Every time you check your phone, your brain can need over twenty minutes to fully lock back in. Do that ten times a day... and the real work never even starts.
So you do the easy stuff. Fast. Loud. All day. Because easy stuff can never tell you that you failed.
Meanwhile, someone else does one hard thing. Ninety minutes. Phone in another room.
One year later... guess who's ahead.
Here's the twist. They're not two people. They're both you. And every morning, you pick which one shows up.
So tomorrow, before one email, one message, one meeting... do the thing you've been avoiding.
Because if you don't, you'll stay busy. And being busy... is the laziest thing you will ever do.
