The most dangerous word in your life isn't "no." It's "Monday."
Everyone has a Monday. "I'll start the diet Monday." "I'll quit Monday." "New me... Monday."
Here's the math nobody tells you. If you live to eighty, you get about four thousand Mondays. Total.
At thirty, you've already burned over fifteen hundred of them... waiting for one.
Two guys. Same goal. Same January.
One waits for the perfect Monday. Clean calendar. New notebook. Perfect plan.
The other starts on a Thursday. At eleven p.m. With ten push-ups and no plan at all.
A year later... guess who's still waiting.
Monday was never the problem. Monday is a hiding place. It lets you feel like a winner without doing anything.
So here's the rule. If it matters, it starts today. Small. Ugly. Badly. Today.
Because tomorrow becomes Monday. And Monday... becomes never.
