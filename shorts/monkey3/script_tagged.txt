[excited] Heaven sent me a job offer. Me. The monkey who erased his own death.
[storytelling] The Jade Emperor smiled. "Welcome! You'll be... Keeper of the Heavenly Horses."
[proud] I had a fancy hat. A fancy desk. A thousand horses. I was so proud.
[laughs] Then a guard laughed. "That's the lowest job in heaven. You're a stable boy."
[deadpan] Stable. Boy.
[angry] I kicked over the desk, flew home, and made a giant flag: "Great Sage, Equal to Heaven."
[cocky] Heaven sent an army. I sent them back... upside down.
[sarcastic] So the Emperor tried again. "Fine! Guard my Peach Garden."
[whispers] These peaches ripen once every three thousand years. Eat one... and you live forever.
[mischievously] I ate one. Then another. Then... all of them.
[laughing] Then I found a party nobody invited me to. So I drank all their wine.
[storytelling] Then I wandered into Laozi's lab and ate his pills of immortality. Like candy.
[sleepy] I woke up. Burped. [nervous] And the sky turned gold.
[ominous] A hundred thousand soldiers were standing around my mountain.
