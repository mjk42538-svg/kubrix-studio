I was born from a rock. Literally. And three hundred years later, I beat up heaven.
But let me start at the beginning.
On top of Flower Fruit Mountain sat a magic stone. One day... it cracked open. And out popped me. A monkey. Made of stone.
When I opened my eyes, two beams of light shot into the sky. So bright, the Jade Emperor, the ruler of heaven, spilled his tea.
He looked down and said: "Eh. It's just a monkey." Big mistake.
The other monkeys found a giant waterfall and made a deal: whoever jumps through it... becomes king.
Everyone was scared. So I jumped.
Behind the water was a secret cave. Stone beds, stone bowls, stone everything.
And just like that... I was the Monkey King.
We partied for three hundred years. Peaches, games, zero homework.
Then I noticed my oldest friend getting really, really old. And I learned the scariest word in the world: death.
So I made a promise. I would never die.
I built a raft and sailed for ten years to find a master who knew the secret of living forever.
I found him on a misty mountain. Master Subodhi.
He named me Sun Wukong. It means "the monkey who understands emptiness." I still don't understand it.
One night, he taught me his biggest secrets. Seventy-two transformations... and the cloud somersault. One flip, and I could fly farther than around the whole world.
So of course, I showed off. I turned into a pine tree in front of everyone.
Master was furious. "Leave! And never tell anyone I was your teacher!"
Then he said one more thing. "Your pride will trap you under a mountain one day."
I laughed. And flew home in one somersault.
But when I landed... my mountain was empty. Every single monkey... was gone.
