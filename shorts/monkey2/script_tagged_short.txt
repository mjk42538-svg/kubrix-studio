[urgent] Last time, every monkey was gone. All I found were claw prints... bigger than my whole body.
[storytelling] I followed them to a cave. Inside: the Demon King of Havoc.
[laughs] He laughed. "You're the Monkey King? You're the size of a peanut!"
[cocky] So I pulled out my own hair... and blew. Every hair turned into a tiny me. Hundreds of them.
[laughing] They pinched him, poked him... and I bonked him with his own sword.
[happy] I saved every monkey. But I needed a real weapon.
[storytelling] Under the ocean, the Dragon King kept a treasure no one could lift.
[sarcastic] He gave me a sword. Too light. A spear. Too light. A giant fork. Snapped it like a breadstick.
[nervous] Then the palace started shaking. A golden iron pillar. Glowing. Waiting for me.
[whispers] Eight tons. And it could change size.
[shouting] "Smaller!" A needle. "Bigger!" It touched the ocean floor.
[cocky] I tucked it behind my ear. "Thanks. I'll take it."
[ominous] That night, two men in black chained me and dragged me to the Underworld.
[shocked] They opened the Book of Life and Death. My name: "Sun Wukong. Dies... tonight."
[angry] So I grabbed their brush... and crossed it out. Mine, and every monkey's. Forever.
[ominous whisper] Up in heaven, the Jade Emperor slammed down his tea. "Bring me... that monkey."
